Meet Your Physiotherapist

Dr. Anvi Kadakia (PT)

Physiotherapist · MPT (Musculoskeletal) · 7+ Years Experience

Dr. Anvi Kadakia is a dedicated physiotherapist with over 7 years of clinical experience in rehabilitation and patient care. She completed her Master's in Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy from NMIMS, Mumbai, and has worked extensively with patients across orthopaedic, sports, neurological, cardiopulmonary, women's health, paediatric, and geriatric rehabilitation.

Currently associated with Kapadia Multispeciality Hospital, Dr. Anvi collaborates with orthopaedic specialists to provide comprehensive rehabilitation care. She believes in identifying the root cause of each patient's problem and designing individualised treatment plans that help patients recover safely and return to their daily activities with confidence.

New Patients

What to Expect on Your First Visit

Your first session is a thorough, personalised assessment — not a rushed appointment.

01
Detailed Assessment

We conduct a thorough evaluation of your condition, medical history, pain levels, posture, movement patterns, and functional limitations — understanding you as a whole.

02
Root-Cause Diagnosis

Dr. Anvi identifies the underlying reason for your pain or dysfunction — not just the symptoms — using clinical reasoning and hands-on assessment.

03
Personalised Treatment Plan

A customised rehabilitation plan is developed with clear recovery goals, treatment techniques, and home care recommendations tailored specifically to you.

Your first session includes:

  • Posture & movement analysis
  • Strength & flexibility testing
  • Pain & functional assessment
  • Review of medical history & reports
  • Root-cause diagnosis discussion
  • Personalised treatment plan
  • Home exercise recommendations
⏱ Duration: Approximately 45 – 60 minutes
